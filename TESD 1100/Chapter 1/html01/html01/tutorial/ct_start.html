Street-Side Thai Flavor

Curbside Thai brings the spice and aroma of Thailand directly to Charlotte with Chef Sajja Adulet’s award-winning dishes served fresh from our modern mobile food truck.

Track us throughout Uptown Charlotte from 11 a.m. to late evening and enjoy bold curries, crisp salads, and stir-fries prepared when you order. We can even deliver to your desk or cater your next event.
