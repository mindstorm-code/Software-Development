The Normandy Invasion

The invasion on the beaches of Normandy on June 6, 1944, was the largest air, land, and sea operation undertaken before or since that date. Operation Overlord involved over 150,000 service men stationed on over 5,000 ships and 11,000 airplanes.

The landing beaches were intended to provide footholds that would allow rapid reinforcement and expansion inland before the enemy could mount a major counter-attack.

Supporting the beach landings, especially at Omaha Beach, airborne divisions descended behind enemy lines, helping ensure that the beach assaults were successful.
